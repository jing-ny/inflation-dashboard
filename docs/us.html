Federal Reserve Inflation Target

The Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC) judges that inflation of 2 percent over the longer run, as measured by the annual change in the price index for personal consumption expenditures (PCE), is most consistent with the Federal Reserve's mandate for maximum employment and price stability.

The Committee seeks to achieve inflation that averages 2 percent over time. When inflation has been running persistently below or above 2 percent, the FOMC will aim to achieve inflation moderately above or below 2 percent for some time.

Source: Federal Reserve, Statement on Longer-Run Goals and Monetary Policy Strategy (amended January 2024)
