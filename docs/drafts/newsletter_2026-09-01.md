# Inflation Newsletter — 2026-09-01

Five of the eleven series we track rose in July; three fell by a material margin. Whatever else is true, the disinflation trade is no longer a single global story.

The upside moves are small and remarkably synchronised. The **Euro Area** went 2.73% → 2.93% (+0.2pp), the **UK** 2.6% → 2.9% (+0.3pp), **Canada** 2.8% → 3.03% (+0.23pp), **Singapore** 1.9% → 2.2% (+0.3pp). **Japan** rose 1.7% → 2.0% (+0.3pp) — back at the BoJ's target after a soft June. None of these is dramatic on its own. Together they put four advanced economies within a whisker of 3%.

Cutting the other way: **China** fell 1.0% → 0.5% (−0.5pp), the weakest reading in the set and half of where it sat in June. **South Korea** dropped 3.16% → 2.79% (−0.37pp), back inside the BOK's comfort range. **South Africa** posted the largest single move, 5.0% → 4.3% (−0.7pp), against a SARB (May 2026) projection of 4.4% for the year.

The **US** (3.36%, −0.17pp) and **Australia** (3.5%, −0.3pp) both edged lower but not by enough to call a turn. **New Zealand** is still on Q1 data at 3.1%, unchanged from Q4.

The April 2026 WEO carries global inflation at 4.4% for the year, with broad upside revisions across advanced economies (US +0.8pp, EA +0.7pp, UK +0.7pp, AU/NZ +1.0pp) attributed to energy pass-through. The per-country numbers leave two visible gaps with central banks: the IMF has Australia at 4.0% against the RBA's 3.3% (August 2026), and the Euro Area at 2.6% against the ECB's 3.0% (June 2026). Next WEO: October.

**What to watch.** China is the live one — a 0.5% July print against an IMF full-year forecast of 1.2% requires a second half that looks nothing like the first. Second, the BoJ (July, rate 0.75%) said CPI would dip below 2% temporarily before rising on energy costs; July's 2.0% arrives earlier than that framing implies, and the Bank has said it will keep raising if the outlook holds. Third, the BoE is publishing scenarios rather than a central path (April MPR, 3.1–3.6% for 2026) — a second consecutive UK upside surprise would start narrowing that fan by itself.
