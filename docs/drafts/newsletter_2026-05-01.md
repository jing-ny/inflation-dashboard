# Inflation Newsletter — 2026-05-01

The eurozone delivered March's sharpest inflation surprise, jumping 0.7pp to 2.6% — the largest single-month move among major economies. That uptick brings European price growth exactly in line with the ECB's revised 2026 forecast of 2.6%, published this month after Middle East energy disruptions forced sharp upward revisions.

Canada followed closely with a 0.6pp rise to 2.4%, while Mexico spiked 0.82pp to 4.59% — its highest reading since late 2025. These moves contrast sharply with Brazil's continued deceleration, falling nearly a full percentage point to 4.14%, and China's slide to just 1.0% as deflationary pressures persist despite policy stimulus.

**Energy shock revisions across the board**

April's IMF World Economic Outlook captures the oil-price impact from Middle East tensions, lifting global inflation forecasts to 4.4% for 2026. The revisions are particularly severe for advanced economies: the US sees a 0.8pp upward adjustment to 3.2%, matching the eurozone's 0.7pp increase to 2.6%. Australia and New Zealand face 1.0pp hikes to 4.0% and 3.1% respectively. The Fund warns a severe-scenario alternative could push global inflation above 6%.

The forecast divergences tell the story. Mexico's Banxico projects 3.8% inflation while the IMF sees 3.9% — relatively aligned despite the recent spike. Brazil's central bank forecast of 5.7% sits well above the IMF's 4.0%, reflecting persistent domestic pressures that February's Selic hike to 14.25% aims to contain. China presents the opposite problem: the IMF's 1.2% projection looks optimistic against March's 1.0% print and ongoing property sector weakness.

**What to watch**

Three tensions demand attention: whether eurozone inflation holds near the ECB's 2.6% forecast after March's jump; if Mexico's surge past 4.5% forces Banxico to pause its cutting cycle; and whether China's sub-1% reading signals deeper demand destruction than Beijing's stimulus can offset. The next major test comes with April data across G7 economies, due mid-May.
