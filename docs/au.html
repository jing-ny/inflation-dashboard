RBA Inflation Target

The Reserve Bank of Australia's inflation target is to keep consumer price inflation between 2 and 3 per cent, on average, over time. This formulation allows for flexibility in the short term while maintaining price stability over the medium term.

The RBA focuses on underlying inflation (trimmed mean) as the best measure of inflation trends, as it removes volatile price movements.

Source: RBA Statement on the Conduct of Monetary Policy
