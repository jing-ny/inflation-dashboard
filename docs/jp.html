Economic Context

Japan experienced decades of low inflation and deflation, struggling to reach its 2% target despite aggressive monetary easing. The Bank of Japan maintained negative interest rates from 2016 until March 2024, when global inflation pressures finally pushed Japanese inflation above target. In 2024, the BoJ ended its negative interest rate policy for the first time in 17 years, marking a historic shift in Japanese monetary policy.
