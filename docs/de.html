Monetary Policy

As a member of the Euro Area, Germany's monetary policy is set by the European Central Bank (ECB). The ECB sets interest rates for the entire Euro Area and targets HICP inflation of 2% over the medium term.

For ECB inflation forecasts and detailed projections, see the Euro Area page.
