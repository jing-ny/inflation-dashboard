New Inflation Target

In July 2025, the SARB announced a preference for inflation to settle at 3%, aiming for the bottom of the current 3-6% target range. This represents a shift toward permanently lower inflation.

SARB Inflation Target

The South African Reserve Bank's inflation target range is 3 to 6 percent, with a midpoint objective of 4.5%. The target is set in consultation with the National Treasury.

In July 2025, the MPC announced a new preference for inflation to stabilize at 3% (the bottom of the target band) to create more monetary policy space and lock in sustainably lower interest rates.
