Policy Updates

Recent changes to South Africa's monetary policy framework

November 12, 2025
New Inflation Target Announced

Finance Minister Enoch Godongwana and SARB Governor Lesetja Kganyago jointly announced a new inflation target of 3% with a ±1 percentage point tolerance band, replacing the 25-year-old 3-6% target range. The new target aims to anchor lower inflation expectations and reduce borrowing costs over the medium term.

November 20, 2025
Rate Cut to 6.75%

The MPC unanimously reduced the repo rate by 25bp to 6.75%, the sixth consecutive cut since September 2024. The SARB noted that if price setters adopt the 3% target, South Africa will have room to reach lower rates faster.

January 20, 2026
On Track for 3% Target

Governor Kganyago stated at Davos that inflation is on course to meet the new 3% target in 2026, ahead of the original 2027 timeline. Full-year 2025 inflation expected between 3.2-3.4%, with 2026 averaging around 3.6%.
